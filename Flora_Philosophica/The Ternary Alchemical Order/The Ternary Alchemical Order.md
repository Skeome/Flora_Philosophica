# The Ternary Alchemical Order (TTAO)

The Ternary Alchemical Order is a real-world academy/organization that makes a "cameo" in the game as a secret society of alchemists. 
They act as the hint system and player guide by "overseeing operations from afar."

## Lore & Interactions

- **Disguised Members:** TTAO members occasionally visit the cabin as customers. To the untrained eye, they appear as normal NPCs, but players can identify them because their dialogue text is rendered in the special alchemical UI font (*Archemy.otf*).
- **Triggered Hints:** Letters from the Order are not time-gated, but rather triggered by specific player actions or failure states.
  - *Example:* The very first letter arrives via the Mailbox only after the player has used the Compost Bin 3 times, giving them the cryptic hint: *"The spent plant is not waste, marry it with fire and water until it is pure."*
- **The Seal:** Their correspondence is sealed with the Order's wax stamp (Solve Et Coagula).
